# BLAU — Five Style Directions, with references for each

_Compiled 2026-08-21. **✔ inspected** = I fetched and read the site for this doc.
**○ candidate** = from search or prior knowledge, worth opening but unverified._

Five directions BLAU could credibly go. Each is a coherent world — palette, typeface,
photography treatment, and pacing all move together. Mixing two of them is how sites end up
looking generic, so the point of this document is to **pick one**.

Every direction is costed against the same brief: one walk-in eatery in Kelapa Gading,
75 menu items, a real coffee program, WhatsApp as the primary CTA, and an existing indigo
identity (`#2D283D` ink, `#414889` heading blue).

---

## A · Editorial Quiet

> Whitespace is the design. Type is small and refined. Photography is full-bleed and rare.
> The restraint itself signals quality.

### References
| Site | Status | What it demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| [Locavore NXT](https://locavorenxt.com/) | ✔ inspected | Monochrome logo, nature-led full-bleed imagery, progressive disclosure — sections expand instead of dumping. **Your own pick.** |
| [Atomix](https://www.atomixnyc.com/) | ✔ inspected | Centred logo, four nav links, black on white, near-zero decoration. Proof a whole two-Michelin restaurant fits on essentially one page. |
| [Sunday in Brooklyn](https://www.sundayinbrooklyn.com/) | ✔ inspected | Closest to BLAU's scale. Neutral palette, photography carries everything, hours split by meal period (breakfast / brunch / midday / dinner), Resy embedded inline. |
| [Noma](https://noma.dk/) | ○ candidate (403'd my fetch) | The reference everyone cites for whitespace-as-luxury. Clean editorial layout, refined type, full-bleed food photography doing all the work. |

### BLAU in this direction
Near-white ground. `#414889` used once, maybe twice, on the whole page. Wordmark small and
centred. A single full-bleed photograph of the Grilled Oxtail. Menu set at 15–16px with a lot
of leading. Hours and address as plain text, not in a card.

**Type recipe:** a light serif or a refined neutral sans at small sizes. Big leading, tight measure.
**Risk:** BLAU is a 38k-coffee neighbourhood eatery. Executed too coldly this reads as
expensive and unwelcoming, and the family/kids-meal segment bounces.
**Verdict: partial fit** — right instincts, wrong temperature if taken neat.

## B · Warm Narrative

> Texture, story, and appetite. The site is busy on purpose. Culture is the differentiator,
> not minimalism.

### References
| Site | Status | What it demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| [Dishoom](https://www.dishoom.com/) | ✔ inspected | **The strongest single reference in this whole document.** All-day eatery, Awwwards-winning. Menus split by meal period as separate routed pages. Persistent "Book a table" CTA. Per-location founding myths set in different moments of Indian history. Warm, photo-dominant, culturally specific. |
| [Ottolenghi](https://ottolenghi.co.uk/) | ✔ inspected | Serif wordmark against sans body, modular card grid, horizontal-scroll collections. Colour enters **only through photography** — terracottas, greens, golds — never through graphic elements. "Refined casualness." |
| [Kopi Kalyan](https://kopikalyan.com/) | ○ candidate | Closest business-model match in Jakarta: coffee *and* a real food menu, vintage-eclectic warmth, own roasting. |

### BLAU in this direction
Warm off-white ground, indigo as the anchor rather than the star. Photography wall-to-wall.
The homepage tells the actual story: one kitchen cooking sambal matah, carbonara, gochujang and
katsudon, and doing pork properly in Kelapa Gading. Menu pages split *Rice Bowls / Pasta & Pizza /
Small Plates / Coffee / Cold Drinks*, each with its own URL.

**Type recipe:** a characterful serif for display against a plain sans for body.
**Risk:** needs volume — a lot of photography and a lot of written copy. BLAU currently has
food shots only and no story text. This direction is the most work.
**Verdict: strong fit, highest effort.**

## C · Cool Precision

> Swiss grid, cold neutrals, type doing the work. The coffee-professional aesthetic.

### References
| Site | Status | What it demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| [%Arabica](https://arabica.com/) | ✔ inspected | The `% ΔRΔBICΔ` mark with geometric deltas. High-contrast greyscale, sparse architecture, five nav links (Philosophy, Locations, Shop, Films, Contact), video-forward. Premium through subtraction. |
| [Common Grounds](https://commongrounds.co.id/) | ✔ inspected | Jakarta. Dark minimal logo, neutral premium palette, and crucially: **Roastery and Archive as their own pages**. Proves a coffee program is real by giving it a room. |
| [Onyx Coffee Lab](https://onyxcoffeelab.com/) | ✔ inspected | Off-whites `#eee9df` / `#fbfaf3`, deep black text, dramatic letter-spaced all-caps headings, alternating left/right hero sections. Each coffee gets flavour notes, producer, origin geography. |
| [1/15 Coffee](https://satuperlimabelas.com/) | ○ candidate | Scandinavian-inspired Jakarta chain. Closest visual cousin to BLAU's cool palette. |

### BLAU in this direction
Strict grid. Letter-spaced all-caps labels. Indigo as a system colour — rules, active states,
the wordmark — not as decoration. The V60 guest-bean program gets its own page with origin and
tasting notes, treated as seriously as Onyx treats theirs.

**Type recipe:** one neutral grotesk across the whole site, differentiated by weight, case and
tracking rather than by family.
**Risk:** cold. BLAU's menu is *fun* — Kopi Susu Bro, Black Magic, Bagoge. A Swiss grid
flattens that personality, and the food half of the business ends up feeling secondary to coffee.
**Verdict: partial fit** — excellent for the coffee pages, wrong for the whole site.

## D · Cinematic Dark

> Dark ground, video first, atmosphere over information.

### References
| Site | Status | What it demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| [ISMAYA](https://www.ismaya.com/) | ✔ inspected | Dark/neutral grounds, monochrome logo, **video backgrounds in section headers**, brands sorted into mood pillars (Elevate, Unite, Ignite, Everyday, Wellbeing) rather than by cuisine. **Your own pick.** |
| [%Arabica](https://arabica.com/) | ✔ inspected | Also lives here — a dedicated *Films* section in the primary nav, with a prominent video player on the homepage. |

### BLAU in this direction
`#2D283D` — the actual menu-cover ink — as the site ground, not just the cover. Wordmark
reversed in white, exactly as the printed menu already does it. Hero is footage of the bar:
V60 pouring, sea salt foam going onto a latte. Menu categories as mood tiles.

**Type recipe:** clean sans reversed out, generous tracking, restrained sizes.
**Risk:** two real ones. Food photography **does not read well on dark grounds** — BLAU's
entire library is shot on white plaster and would need reshooting or heavy treatment. And
there is currently no video at all.
**Verdict: blocked** — the best-looking option on paper, but it depends on assets that don't exist.

## E · Graphic Block

> Oversized type, flat colour fields, hard edges. The fast-casual challenger look.

### References
| Site | Status | What it demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| **BLAU's own 2025 menu** | ✔ inspected | This is the honest one. Big indigo headings, flat white fields, no rules, no ornament, photography floated in white space. **BLAU already designed in this style.** |
| [Kopi Kenangan](https://www.kopikenangan.com/) | ✔ inspected | Indonesian conventions done at scale: themed drink "series" as merchandising slots, outlet finder with Maps, WhatsApp as consumer service. |
| Sweetgreen / Nando's / Feastables | ○ candidate | The fast-casual camp identified in 2026 trend coverage — custom typography and illustration systems used to escape category defaults. Feastables in particular: playful product blocks, oversized type, vivid fills. |

Per current design-press coverage, restaurant web design in 2026 has split cleanly into two
camps: fine dining leaning on **editorial restraint** where the typeface alone carries the
positioning, and fast-casual leaning on **custom type and colour systems** to avoid looking
like every other site in the category. Direction A is the first camp. This is the second.

### BLAU in this direction
`#414889` at display scale — headings large enough to crop. Flat indigo panels alternating
with white. Menu categories as full-width colour blocks. Prices set large and tabular. The
cut-through `A` in the wordmark pulled out and used as a graphic device.

**Type recipe:** one heavy geometric sans at display scale against a plain sans for body.
**Risk:** can tip into loud and cheap if the photography isn't strong. BLAU's photography is
strong.
**Verdict: strong fit, lowest effort** — it is a direct translation of the identity that already exists.

## Recommendation

**E as the frame, B as the voice.**

Direction E is not a style I would be imposing on BLAU — it is the style BLAU's own menu is
already designed in. Big indigo headings, flat white, generous air, photography floating free.
Carrying that to the web means the site and the printed menu are visibly the same brand, and
it is the only direction fully buildable with the assets that exist today.

Then borrow B's substance where E is thin: Dishoom's menu architecture (separate routed pages
cut by how people eat, persistent CTA) and Dishoom's willingness to tell a specific cultural
story rather than a generic one.

What that avoids: every other Jakarta cafe defaults to warm brown-and-cream. **Nobody in this
category is using indigo at display scale.** It is the cheapest available way to look like nobody else.

**The one question that decides it:** do you want BLAU to read *quiet and considered* (A/C) or
*warm and appetising* (B/E)? They produce genuinely different sites and I'd rather you pick
than have me guess.
